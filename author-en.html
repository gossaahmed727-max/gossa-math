Scientific Archive (History of Work)

2011 — Proportional Calculus

Initial discovery of relative differentiation, where the derivative is redefined to measure proportional shifts rather than absolute increments.

D_{p} f(x) = \frac{x}{f(x)} \cdot \frac{df}{dx}
2012 — Logarithmic Calculus

Extended the research into higher-order tiers, providing the mathematical tools to analyze systems governed by logarithmic growth patterns.
