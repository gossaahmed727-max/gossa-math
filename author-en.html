Professional Background

Eng. Ahmed Gossa is a licensed Civil Engineer accredited by the Algerian Ministry of Housing, Urban Planning and the City. Based in the province of Biskra (Bentious, M'khadma), his professional practice involves structural analysis and complex infrastructure design.

His unique dual path integrates rigorous engineering methodology with high-level mathematical theory, leading to the development of a structured framework for measuring multi-order change.
